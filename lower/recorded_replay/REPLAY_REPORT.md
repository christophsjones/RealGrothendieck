## What is established about 1.775

`FROZEN_ROW_LIMIT.json` is a separate directed certificate, not an upper bound
on the true Grothendieck constant. At the scalar coordinate x=381/400, the
valid sign witness h(s)=sign(s), k(s)=0 proves that each of the 35 frozen rows,
even with its exact optimal universal fibre constant and zero repair budget,

Consequently tightening only those constants cannot reach 1.775, whose
